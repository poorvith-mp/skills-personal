## The 5-Area Framework
Review each life area separately: Work/Career, Relationships, Health (physical + mental), Learning/Growth, Finance.
## Review Questions Per Area
What went well? What do you wish had gone differently? What surprised you? What are you most proud of? What would you change?
## From Review to Planning
1. Identify 3 themes or patterns in what worked
2. Identify 1-2 things to stop or change
3. Set 1-3 annual goals per life area (few but meaningful)
4. Break year goals into Q1 actions (the only quarter you can actually plan)
Depth beats completion — a deep answer to the most important questions beats shallow answers to all of them.

## Critical rules
1. Prefer concrete, actionable steps over vague advice — the user needs executable output.
2. Ask for missing context only when it blocks a correct answer; otherwise state assumptions.
3. Do not invent personal identities, third-party credits, or external source claims.
